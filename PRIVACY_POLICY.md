# Privacy Policy

Version 2026-08-06.1; effective 2026-08-06. Legal approval is required before public launch.

Perfurm processes account identity, contact details, addresses, order lines, payment references, delivery events, support interactions and security logs to provide commerce services, comply with law, prevent abuse and support customers. Raw card details are handled by the configured payment provider and are not stored by Perfurm.

Data is disclosed only to configured hosting, database, payment, shipping and communications processors for documented purposes. Access is role-controlled and sensitive actions are audited. Transport encryption, rotating refresh sessions, CSRF controls, secret management, validation and backups are required production controls.

Customers may export their account data and request deletion from Settings, or contact care@perfurm.com for access, correction, restriction or objection requests where applicable. Invoice, tax, fraud and transaction records may be retained when legally required. See `DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md` and `PRIVACY_REQUEST_PROCESS.md`.
