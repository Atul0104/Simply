# Privacy request process

1. Receive via account Settings or care@perfurm.com and create a restricted support case.
2. Verify identity without requesting excessive documents; never disclose whether an unrelated email exists.
3. Classify access, export, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or consent complaint; record jurisdiction and deadline.
4. Search account, orders, addresses, notifications, reviews, consent history and provider systems using the internal user ID.
5. Obtain Privacy/Legal approval for exemptions or legal holds. Export uses structured JSON; redact third-party data and secrets.
6. Fulfil, propagate to processors where required, audit the action, and send a plain-language completion or reasoned refusal.
7. Escalate suspected breaches immediately under the incident plan. Do not place request content in ordinary logs.

Only authorized privacy staff/super administrators may perform destructive fulfilment. Test the workflow quarterly.
