# Cookie Policy

Version 2026-08-06.1; effective 2026-08-06. This operational draft requires qualified legal review before production.

Perfurm uses necessary technology for secure sign-in, fraud prevention, checkout, consent evidence and the shopping cart. Optional functional, analytics, marketing and personalization technology remains disabled until a visitor chooses it. Necessary technology cannot be disabled because the requested service would fail.

Consent lasts no more than 180 days and is requested again after a material version change. Visitors can reopen **Cookie preferences** from the footer or account Settings, reject optional categories, or withdraw previous consent. Withdrawal stops future optional collection and clears known optional browser identifiers; lawful transaction/security records follow the retention policy. Global Privacy Control is honored by keeping analytics, marketing and personalization off.

The authoritative technical list, vendors, purposes and lifetimes is in `COOKIE_INVENTORY.md`. Contact care@perfurm.com for privacy questions.
